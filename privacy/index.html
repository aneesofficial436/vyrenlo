Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Overview

Vyrenlo (“we,” “us”) operates vyrenlo.com and related services. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use our website, contact form, or scheduling tools, and how we use it.

This page is provided for transparency on our marketing site. It is not legal advice. If you engage us for a project, additional terms may apply under a separate agreement.

Information we collect

Information you provide voluntarily, such as your name, email address, company name, and message content when you submit our contact form or book a meeting.

Scheduling information you enter in Calendly (or similar tools) linked from our site, such as name, email, and meeting preferences.

Technical data our hosting and analytics providers may process, such as IP address, browser type, device information, and pages visited. Cloudflare may process such data to deliver and secure the site.

How we use information

To respond to inquiries, schedule calls, and evaluate potential client engagements.

To operate, maintain, and improve our website and security.

To comply with applicable law and protect our rights, users, and the public.

We do not sell your personal information.

Third-party services

We use trusted processors to run the site, including Cloudflare (hosting and security), Formspree (contact form delivery), and Calendly (appointment scheduling). Their use of data is governed by their respective privacy policies.

Links on our site may lead to third-party websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

Retention

We retain contact and business correspondence for as long as needed to respond, maintain client relationships, and meet legal or accounting obligations, unless you ask us to delete information where we are not required to retain it.

Your choices

You may request access, correction, or deletion of personal information we hold about you by emailing contact@vyrenlo.com. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling a request.

You can disable cookies in your browser; some site features may not function optimally.

International visitors

If you access our site from outside the country where we operate, your information may be processed in jurisdictions where our service providers host data.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the site after changes means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact@vyrenlo.com or info@vyrenlo.com.
